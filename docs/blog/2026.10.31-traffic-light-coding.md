---
title: NSBE at Montgomery Catholic STEAMtopia
---

On October 31, 2026, National Society of Black Engineers Montgomery 
Professionals will be Montgomery Catholic for their STEAMtopia event.

We will have several exhibits on display. Our members will be there to 
talk about STEM and the various outreaches that we do in the community, 
including our [NSBE Jr Program](/cansbejr).
